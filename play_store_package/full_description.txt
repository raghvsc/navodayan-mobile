Welcome to Navodayan, the official mobile platform connecting alumni of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) across India and worldwide.

Whether you graduated decades ago or recently passed out, Navodayan empowers you to reconnect with batchmates, discover alumni in your city or industry, give back to your school, and grow through mentorship and community collaboration.

🌟 KEY FEATURES:

🎓 Verified Alumni Directory
• Search and filter alumni by JNV school, passing batch, graduation year, profession, industry, and city.
• View verified alumni profiles highlighting academic background, professional experience, and mentorship interests.

🤝 1-on-1 Mentorship & Career Guidance
• Connect with senior Navodayans across diverse fields (Technology, Medicine, Civil Services, Law, Entrepreneurship, Research, Arts).
• Book guidance sessions, seek career advice, and mentor current students and junior alumni.

💡 Navodayan CARE Sessions
• Join alumni-led workshops and interactive knowledge-sharing sessions on competitive examinations, tech careers, startup building, and skill development.

📅 Community Events & Virtual Lounges
• Discover national alumni meets, regional gatherings, batch reunions, and virtual webinars.
• Join community lounges to collaborate with peers sharing common goals and interests.

💬 Safe & Private Messaging
• Send connection requests and direct messages to fellow Navodayans.
• Comprehensive safety controls: built-in user blocking and content/message reporting for a secure, respectful environment.

🏛️ School Support & Giving Back
• Track alumni initiatives, school development funds, and volunteer contributions supporting your alma mater.

🔒 Privacy First & Data Control
• Complete control over your information: download your complete data archive anytime or permanently delete your account directly within settings.

Join the Navodayan family today and strengthen the bond that unites us across batches, states, and generations!
